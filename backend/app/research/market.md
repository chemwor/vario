## Goal

Size and describe the market for **{product}** well enough to decide whether, and where, to push. {focus}

## How to work

1. Define the market narrowly from the product context: the specific customer who would pay for {product}
   and the job they'd hire it for. A broad industry number ("the $400B software market") is useless; say so
   if that's all you can find.
2. Estimate size two ways and show your arithmetic:
   - **top-down:** analyst or industry figures, cut down to the slice that fits;
   - **bottom-up:** number of target customers × a realistic price (use competitors' real prices).
3. Find who actually pays (the user, their boss, a company card?), how they find tools like this, and how long
   they take to buy.
4. Find growth signals: search trends, job postings, funding in the space, regulation, platform shifts.
5. Prefer primary sources (government statistics, company filings, pricing pages) over SEO reports. When a
   number comes from a paywalled report, say so and treat it as low-confidence.
