## Goal

Map the competition for **{product}** so the team building it knows exactly who they're up against and where
they can win. {focus}

## How to work

1. From the product context, write down in one line what problem {product} solves and for whom. Research
   against that, not against the product's exact feature list.
2. Find **direct competitors** (same problem, same customer), **indirect** ones (same problem, different
   approach or customer) and the **"do nothing" option** (spreadsheets, manual work, an in-house script).
3. For the 5–8 most relevant, open their own site and pricing page. Don't rely on listicles for prices.
4. Look for what their users complain about: G2 / Capterra / App Store reviews, Reddit, Hacker News, their
   public GitHub issues. Complaints are where the opportunities are.
5. Note anything recent: launches, price changes, funding, shutdowns (with dates).
