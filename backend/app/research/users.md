## Goal

Hear the people **{product}** is for, in their own words: what hurts, what they do about it today, what
they'd pay for, and what would make them say no. {focus}

## How to work

1. From the product context, write down who the end user is and what they'd be complaining about *before*
   they've heard of {product}. Search for the problem, not the product.
2. Search Reddit first (`site:reddit.com <problem words>`; find the subreddits where these people actually
   talk), then Hacker News, niche forums, Stack Exchange, product review sites and GitHub issues.
3. Open the threads and read the comments, not just titles. If a Reddit page won't load, try the same URL on
   old.reddit.com.
4. Collect **verbatim quotes** (short, with links). Group them into themes and count roughly how often each
   theme comes up, and how strongly.
5. Note the words people use: they're the words {product}'s landing page and onboarding should use.
6. Never include usernames or anything that identifies a person: quote the words, link the thread.
